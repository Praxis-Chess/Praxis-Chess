Hand-off for designers, developers and AI agents

A chess coach that looks like an instrument, not a game lobby.

Praxis Chess is a self-hosted coach for one player. It syncs their Chess.com games, checks every move with Stockfish, diagnoses each serious mistake, and turns the mistakes into drills, a pattern report and a weekly plan. This page covers how it looks, the rules behind the look, and the path a player takes through it. Every screenshot is the real app rendered with synthetic test data.

01 · Rules

Principles

These are applied in code review, and the Phase 12 Playwright tests assert several of them by name. When a design choice is in doubt, these decide it.

  1. Instrument-panel minimalism

    A warm near-black canvas with translucent panels, hairlines and a crosshair mark on each card. The only decoration is a 3% grain overlay and Prax.

  2. One meaning per colour

    Pink marks only what can be clicked. Amber means above your own average. Green, amber and red carry results and severity. Everything else is grey.

  3. Truthful labels

    "Costliest" means total winning chances lost. A rate is per 100 of your moves. "Rolling avg (up to 10 games)" says that the first points average fewer games.

  4. Compared to what

    Every figure sits next to its baseline: your overall rate or your average per game. Bars carry a baseline tick.

  5. One threshold

    Ten. Below it, a figure is faded and marked "few", and it is never ranked or called the worst.

  6. Account for the whole

    Ranked lists end with "The rest: …". When the same mistakes appear in two lists, the overlap is stated.

  7. One format per fact

    32 of 145 (22%) · 19 blunders · −27% winning chance each. Rates have one decimal. Dates read 6 Sep.

  8. Say each fact once

    Open with one sentence of takeaway, then give the evidence. Each section explains itself once, briefly.

  9. Recognition over recall

    A move is shown with its opening, date and loss. Hover or focus shows the board.

  10. Feedback and state

    Plans show their dates, what is done and what is next. The next action is the only solid pink button on the screen.

  11. Verified or silent

    The language model never states a number the engine didn't measure. Report prose is off by default, and "✓ Verified" appears only when the board proves the claim.

04 · Space

Shape, depth and layout

PropertyRule
SpacingFlex or grid with gap. Page sections 26px apart, items in a section 14–18px apart, lines inside an item 4–6px apart. Cards have 20px of padding, or 16px in dense grids.
RadiiCards and inputs 4px, buttons 3px, badges 2px, chips 10px. Nothing is fully round except the week dots.
DepthTranslucency plus backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(115%). The only shadows are an inset highlight on cards and the opaque board popover.
Card markA small + crosshair at the top right, drawn by ::after. .card.plain drops it for repeated items.
FrameA sticky 52px nav: logo, then Today, Progress, Library, Play, Ask Prax, Insights, and a Settings gear on the right. A SyncStatusBanner strip sits below it. main has a max-width of 1280px, 24px of padding, and a right gutter where Prax lives.
Reading widthContent columns are capped at about 860–960px. Session cards use repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, 250px), 1fr)).
Phone.stack-on-phone puts grids into one column below 760px. A narrow Playwright project runs every spec at phone width.

08 · To fix

Known drift

Found while capturing these screens. Each item breaks one of the principles above, so fix it or record the decision to keep it.

  1. Colour
    The Insights Overview uses pink for data.

    The Missed Tactics bars and the Monday bar are orchid, but pink is reserved for what can be clicked. Use neutral, or amber against a baseline, as the Pattern Report does.

  2. Colour
    Amber has two meanings.

    It is the mistake severity and also "above your average". Either give one of them another colour, or document the double use.

  3. Board
    The report popover board is blue.

    It uses #3d5a80 / #98c1d9, while every other board is bone and graphite. Switch it to --board-light / --board-dark.

  4. Truth
    "Positional" is listed as a missed tactic.

    It shows 154 on the Overview, built from old motif data. The Pattern Report no longer counts OTHER or POSITIONAL as tactics.

  5. Phone
    The nav overflows at 390px.

    Six links don't fit and "Insights" is cut off. A wrapped row, a scroll hint or a menu would fix it.

  6. Type
    Fonts aren't loaded.

    The look depends on what each machine has installed. Self-host Inter Tight and JetBrains Mono.

  7. Copy
    Em-dash copy remains.

    It's in the setup checklist ("Database — PostgreSQL reachable"), the Play subtitle and "Phase recall — last session". That wording was replaced elsewhere.

  8. Settings
    "Written explanations per game" is still shown.

    Per-move explanations are retired. Hide the field or mark it off.

  9. Pattern
    There are two tab styles.

    Library uses an underline and Insights uses a pill. Pick one.
